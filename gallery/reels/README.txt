REELS THAT AUTOPLAY
===================
Put mp4 files here, then list them in config.js under  reels: [ ... ]

They will play silently as the customer scrolls, exactly like Instagram.
Tap once to unmute. Tap the Instagram badge to open the original post.

To get your own reel as an mp4: open your post in the Instagram app ->
three dots -> Save / Download. That works for posts on your own account.

Keep each file under 20 MB (Cloudflare's limit is 25 MB per file).
Vertical 9:16 looks best. A matching .jpg poster makes it load instantly.
