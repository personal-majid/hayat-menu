THE TWO ADDRESSES
=================

Same site, two very different people. The address decides which.

  OUR TABLET      https://personal-majid.github.io/hayat-menu/?us
  A GUEST'S PHONE https://personal-majid.github.io/hayat-menu/

That ?us is the whole trick.


WHAT CHANGES
------------
                          our tablet (?us)      a guest's phone
  Instagram / WhatsApp     shows a QR to scan     opens the app
  Google review            shows a QR to scan     opens Google
  A reel                   shows a QR to scan     opens Instagram
  Full screen              yes, on first touch    no, leave their browser alone
  Star page                #/rate, ends in a QR   #/review, opens Google

The tablet never navigates away from the menu. The guest's phone always
goes straight where they tapped — they are already signed in there.


SETTING UP A TABLET (once per tablet)
-------------------------------------
  1. Chrome -> open   .../hayat-menu/?us
  2. Three-dot menu -> Install app  (or Add to Home screen)
  3. Open it from the home screen icon from now on.

Once a tablet has been opened with ?us it stays in tablet mode for good,
even if someone later opens the plain address on it. To hand a device back
to normal, open it once with  ?guest  on the end.


THE THREE QR CODES
------------------
  Table QR (menu)    .../hayat-menu/           -> guest phone, full menu
  Review card        .../hayat-menu/#/review   -> guest phone, stars
  Tablet             .../hayat-menu/?us        -> only used when setting up

Never print the ?us address on anything a customer can scan. A guest who
lands on it gets QR codes instead of working links.


IF A TABLET IS BEHAVING LIKE A PHONE
------------------------------------
It was opened without ?us before it was ever marked. Open
.../hayat-menu/?us once and it will remember.
