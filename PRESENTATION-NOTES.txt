⚠ TWO THINGS I DID NOT BUILD, AND WHY
=====================================

1. FAKE CELEBRITY VISITOR PHOTOS
   Putting a picture of a public figure on your wall implying they ate
   here, when they did not, is a false endorsement. In India that exposes
   you to a personality-rights claim from the celebrity, and it is the
   kind of thing a local competitor screenshots and reports. It also
   collapses instantly — one person asks "when did they come?" and the
   staff have no answer.

   DO THIS INSTEAD — it works better and it is real:
   - Put up a "Guest Wall". Ask happy customers for a photo at the till.
     Most say yes. Twenty real smiling local families beats one fake
     celebrity, because visitors recognise their neighbours.
   - Invite a genuine Malayalam food vlogger. Many will come for a free
     meal and post honestly. That is a real visit, a real video, and you
     can then legitimately put it on this page.

2. A MADE-UP "BEST MANDI AWARD"
   An invented award is a false claim to customers. If it ever appears on
   your Google listing or a poster it is straightforwardly misleading
   advertising, and it devalues the real things you have.

   YOU ALREADY HAVE BETTER, TRUE CLAIMS:
   - 4.1 stars on Google
   - Reviews that literally say "if you are seeking Arabic food, look no
     further" and "food variety is limited but what is served is
     excellent". Quote those, with the reviewer's first name. Real quotes
     from real people are far more persuasive than a trophy graphic.
   - "Cooked in a traditional Kerala taravadu-style hall" — a real
     reviewer wrote that. Use it.
   - Halal · Slow cooked · Free parking · Family hall — all true, all
     useful.

   REAL AWARDS YOU COULD ACTUALLY WIN THIS YEAR:
   - Zomato / Swiggy district ratings badges (just needs listing + volume)
   - Local newspaper and Malayalam food-page features — they run these
     constantly and usually just need a phone call
   - District hotel and restaurant association listings

   Give me a month of real reviews and I will build you a badge that is
   true.
