CLOUDFLARE PAGES — FIRST TIME ONLY
==================================

You upload this folder yourself. About 10 minutes, costs nothing,
no credit card is asked for.

1. Go to  dash.cloudflare.com  and sign up (email + password).

2. Left menu ->  Workers & Pages.

3. Create  ->  Pages tab  ->  Upload assets.

4. Project name:  hayat-menu
   This becomes your address:  hayat-menu.pages.dev

5. Drag  hayat-cloudflare-deploy.zip  straight into the upload box.
   Cloudflare accepts a zip file or a folder — either works.

6. Deploy site. Wait about a minute. Done.

Live at:  https://hayat-menu.pages.dev


AFTER IT IS LIVE
================

a) Add it to Google.
   Google Maps -> your listing -> Edit profile -> Contact -> Website
   Paste the address. This fills the empty Website field Google is
   currently showing on your listing.

b) Put it in the Instagram bio.

c) QR codes for the tables — any free QR generator, paste the URL.

d) On the tablets: open the URL in Chrome -> three-dot menu ->
   Install app (or Add to Home screen).
   After that it works even if the wifi drops, because the site
   stores itself on the device.


UPDATING LATER
==============

Edit config.js or menu-data.js on your computer, then:
Cloudflare -> your project -> Create deployment -> drag the contents again.

ONE EXTRA STEP. Open sw.js and change this line:

    const CACHE = "hayat-v7";

to hayat-v8, then v9, and so on every time you upload.
That is what tells the tablets to fetch the new version instead of
showing the old cached one. Skip it and your changes will not appear.


WHAT IS IN HERE
===============

menu-data.js            dishes and prices

sw.js                   makes the site work offline once visited

assets/                 logo, app icons, WhatsApp share image
photos/                 drop dish photos here
gallery/                drop restaurant photos and videos here
_headers, robots.txt    server settings, leave them alone
