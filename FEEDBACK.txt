WHERE THE FEEDBACK GOES
=======================

You asked for low ratings to be saved to a text file on the server.

Cloudflare Pages is static hosting — it serves files, it cannot write them.
There is no server-side folder to save into. So the flow works like this
instead, with no backend and nothing to maintain:

1 or 2 or 3 stars
  -> the customer gets a private form (name, phone, what went wrong)
  -> pressing send does TWO things:
       a) saves the entry in that device's browser storage
       b) opens WhatsApp with the whole complaint typed out, addressed
          to 9844326842 — so it reaches you within seconds

4 or 5 stars
  -> Google review QR + a button, because that is where a good review
     is actually worth something

The point of splitting it: bad experiences come to you privately, good
ones go public. That is the single highest-value thing on this site for
your Google ranking.


PARTY ENQUIRIES
===============
"Plan my party" asks for a phone number first, then occasion, date,
headcount and notes. On submit it saves the enquiry on that tablet and
shows a WhatsApp QR so the customer can start the chat from their own
phone. That is how you end up with a real WhatsApp thread — a tablet
sitting on the table cannot message them for you.

WHY IT DOES NOT AUTO-SEND A HELLO
Messaging a customer who has not written to you first requires the
WhatsApp Business Platform (Cloud API): a Meta Business account, business
verification, a phone number that is NOT already on normal WhatsApp, and
pre-approved templates. Doable, first 1,000 conversations a month free,
but a separate week of setup. Until then the enquiry lands on the tablet
and staff call back — which for a party booking works better anyway.


READING WHAT IS SAVED ON A TABLET
=================================
On the tablet, tap the small logo in the top-left corner FIVE times quickly.
It offers to download  hayat-tablet-export.txt  containing every party
enquiry AND every piece of feedback saved on that device. Customers will never find this by accident.

Note: storage is per device and per browser. The tablet on table 1 does not
see what was typed on table 2. WhatsApp is what gives you the single stream.


IF YOU LATER WANT A REAL DATABASE
=================================
Cloudflare Pages Functions plus KV storage does this properly and is free
(100,000 reads a day). It is about 20 lines of code and one binding in the
dashboard. Worth doing only once the feedback volume justifies it — until
then WhatsApp is faster and you will actually read it.
