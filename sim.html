How to read this

Both sides replay the same guests from your bill report, minute by minute. A is how you work today. B is the plan you pick above.

Each square is a table, grouped by area. Its colour says what the guests are waiting for, and the number is minutes since they sat down. A dashed square is empty. The floor maps show the same colours on the plan.

The list under each map is the live order board, oldest and most urgent first, like the expo screen big chains use at the pass.

The scoreboard tracks what chains measure: time to take the order, time from sitting to food on the table, plates left waiting after they are ready, and staff time lost standing at the kitchen.

The panel on the right builds plan B. Pick a step, add or remove staff, or switch single changes on and off. The number next to each change is what it would do to walking and to seat-to-food on this day, starting from your current plan B.

Record video makes a downloadable video of the replay right here in the browser. Keep this tab open until it finishes.
