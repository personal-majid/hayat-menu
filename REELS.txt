ADDING REELS — THIS IS THE WHOLE JOB
====================================

Open config.js, find  trending: [  and paste the link. That is it.

    trending: [
      "https://www.instagram.com/reel/DapjpUJTzuf/",
      "https://www.instagram.com/reel/Db09DWopWC3/",
      "https://www.instagram.com/reel/DZnI_k8Tfo3/",
      "https://www.instagram.com/reel/YOUR_NEW_ONE/",     <- just add lines
      "https://www.instagram.com/p/A_PHOTO_POST_ALSO_WORKS/"
    ],

No QR files to make. No images to upload. Nothing to generate.
Add one or add three hundred — the row scrolls sideways.

Optional: give one a caption instead of the default text —

    { url:"https://www.instagram.com/reel/XXXX/", title:"Friday night rush" },

Plain links and captioned ones can sit in the same list.


WHAT THE CUSTOMER SEES
======================
The real Instagram post renders on the tablet — actual video frame,
caption, everything. Tapping it does NOT open the Instagram app.
It opens a QR code, drawn on the spot, and they watch it on their own
phone. The tablet stays on your menu.

If the restaurant wifi is down or Instagram is unreachable, each card
falls back to a branded play tile. Tapping still gives the QR, because
the QR is generated on the device and needs no internet at all.


WHY NOT JUST PLAY IT ON THE TABLET
==================================
Instagram's embed will not autoplay or play inline on a touch device —
that is Meta's iframe, not something any website can override. Every
restaurant menu has this problem.

If you want video playing ON the tablet:
  - YouTube:  paste a video id into  youtube: [...]  in config.js
  - your mp4: drop it into gallery/reels/ and list it in  reels: [...]
Both play right in the page. See gallery/reels/README.txt.


THE QR CODE GENERATOR
=====================
assets/qr.js draws the codes in the browser. It was checked against a
reference encoder and every code decoded back to the exact URL.
You never need to touch this file.
