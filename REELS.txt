ADDING REELS — THIS IS THE WHOLE JOB
====================================

Open config.js, find  trending: [  and paste the link. That is it.

    trending: [
      "https://www.instagram.com/reel/DapjpUJTzuf/",
      "https://www.instagram.com/reel/Db09DWopWC3/",
      "https://www.instagram.com/reel/DZnI_k8Tfo3/",
      "https://www.instagram.com/reel/YOUR_NEW_ONE/",     <- just add lines
      "https://www.instagram.com/p/A_PHOTO_POST_ALSO_WORKS/"
    ],

No QR files to make. No images to upload. Nothing to generate.
Add one or add three hundred — the row scrolls sideways.

Optional: give one a caption instead of the default text —

    { url:"https://www.instagram.com/reel/XXXX/", title:"Friday night rush" },

Plain links and captioned ones can sit in the same list.


WHAT THE CUSTOMER SEES
======================
The real Instagram post renders on the tablet and PLAYS THERE. It is a
plain iframe pointed at instagram.com/reel/<code>/embed/ — no Meta app,
no developer review, no API key. The post just has to be public.

Each card also carries a small round QR button in the corner. Tapping
that gives a QR code, drawn on the spot, so the guest can carry the
reel to their own phone and follow you. The tablet stays on the menu
either way.

If the restaurant wifi is down or Instagram is unreachable, each card
falls back to a branded play tile. The QR still works — it is generated
on the device and needs no internet at all.

WHAT META DOES AND DOES NOT REQUIRE
===================================
  direct iframe   /embed/          no review, no app        <- what we use
  blockquote + embed.js            no review, auto-sizes
  oEmbed JSON API                  needs a Meta app + oembed_read

We use the first one. Nothing to register, nothing to renew.


WHY IT DOES NOT START BY ITSELF
==============================
The embed plays when the guest taps it. It will not autoplay silently
the way a reel does inside the Instagram app — that is Meta's iframe,
not something any website can override.

If you want video that starts on its own:
  - YouTube:  paste a video id into  youtube: [...]  in config.js
  - your mp4: drop it into gallery/reels/ and list it in  reels: [...]
  - the welcome videos: intro/ — see INTRO-VIDEOS.txt
All three play by themselves.


THE QR CODE GENERATOR
=====================
assets/qr.js draws the codes in the browser. It was checked against a
reference encoder and every code decoded back to the exact URL.
You never need to touch this file.
