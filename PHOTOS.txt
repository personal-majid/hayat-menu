GETTING REAL PHOTOS ONTO THE MENU
=================================

I cannot download images in this environment, and I would not put stock
photos of someone else's mandi next to your prices anyway — customers
notice, and it is a licensing risk. So here are the two honest routes.


ROUTE 1 — SHOOT YOUR OWN (best, one hour)
=========================================
This beats any stock library. Your food already looks better than the
generic pictures, and it is legally yours forever.

WHEN
  Between 3pm and 4pm on a clear day. The lunch rush is over and the
  light is still good.

WHERE
  One table next to the window, side-on to the light. Never under the
  ceiling tube light, never with flash. Flash makes food look grey.

SETUP
  - Wipe the plate rim
  - Dark or wooden surface. A plain tray works
  - Put a white A4 sheet on the shadow side to bounce light back
  - Phone on the highest resolution setting

TWO SHOTS PER DISH
  1. Straight down from above (flat lay)
  2. At about 30 degrees, close, so the steam and texture show

SHOT LIST — 25 photos covers the whole menu
  Signature Mandi     overloaded-mandi, beef-mandi, chicken-mandi
  Alfaham             af-regular, af-peri, af-cheesy, af-green
                      (the other four flavours look near identical,
                       reuse the closest one)
  Shawaya             sh-regular, sh-masala
  Exotic              broast, dragon
  Gravies             g1 butter chicken, g3 kadai, g7 dal fry
  Breads              b3 parota, b1 khubbus
  Shawarma            w1 chicken, w5 jumbo
  Sides               s1 salad
  Drinks              d1 fresh lime, d7 mint mojito, d5 mixed fruit
  Desserts            x2 falooda, x3 mango delight, x4 ice cream

ALSO SHOOT, for the Gallery
  - The dining hall, empty, from the door
  - The mandi pit being opened, steam rising
  - Alfaham on the charcoal
  - The signboard from the road at dusk
  - Your team, one group photo

HOW TO ADD THEM
  1. Resize each to about 1600px wide, under 400 KB
     (free: squoosh.app — drag, drop, download)
  2. Name them exactly like the ids above:
        photos/beef-mandi.jpg
        photos/af-peri.jpg
  3. Open menu-data.js, find that dish, change
        img:""      to      img:"photos/beef-mandi.jpg"
  4. Re-zip, re-upload

Any dish you have not shot keeps its illustration. Nothing breaks.
You can do five dishes a week.


ROUTE 2 — GENUINELY FREE STOCK, IF YOU MUST
===========================================
These three allow commercial use with no attribution required. Read each
site's licence yourself before publishing — they do change.

  pexels.com          the best food photography of the three
  unsplash.com        good for interiors and mood shots
  pixabay.com         biggest library, quality is uneven

SEARCH TERMS THAT ACTUALLY RETURN THE RIGHT FOOD
  mandi rice            kabsa            arabic rice platter
  grilled chicken charcoal               shish tawook
  chicken shawarma wrap                  doner
  butter chicken curry bowl              indian curry
  kerala paratha        flatbread stack  khubz
  fried chicken bucket                   broasted chicken
  mint mojito glass     fresh lime soda  falooda
  mango dessert bowl    ice cream cup

USE THEM FOR
  Backgrounds, the gallery, drinks and desserts — things that look the
  same everywhere.

DO NOT USE THEM FOR
  Your mandi, alfaham, shawaya or the Overloaded Mandi. Those are what
  the customer is paying for and comparing. A stock photo that does not
  match what arrives on the table costs you a bad review.

  Also: never take pictures off another restaurant's Instagram or Google
  listing. That is their copyright and people in Malappuram will spot it.
